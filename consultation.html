The Consultation Process

Expert guidance. Personalized recommendations. A clear path forward.

Every patient has a unique story. Some are seeking answers after years of pain. Some are trying to avoid surgery. Others want to return to sports, optimize hormones, address hair loss, or maintain an active lifestyle as they age. Wherever you are in your journey, the goal is simple: expert evaluation, personalized recommendations, and a clear understanding of your options.

How It Works

Seven steps, zero guesswork

01

Connect With Our Team

The process begins with an inquiry through our office. You'll be connected with a dedicated Patient Liaison — your primary point of contact for scheduling, records, imaging coordination, and questions throughout the process.

02

Upload Imaging & Medical Records

Your Patient Liaison provides a secure upload link for MRI studies, CT scans, X-rays, operative reports, physician notes, and laboratory testing — so Dr. Peaches can review your records before your consultation whenever possible.

03

Virtual Consultation With Dr. Peaches

Most patients begin with a virtual consultation, connecting from anywhere in the country. You'll discuss your symptoms, history, imaging findings, functional limitations, and health goals — and ask any questions you have.

04

Personalized Evaluation & Recommendations

Following a review of your history, symptoms, and imaging, Dr. Peaches discusses recommendations tailored to your situation — spanning orthopedic care, spine and pelvic conditions, regenerative medicine, sports medicine, and wellness & longevity.

05

In-Person Evaluation

If additional evaluation or treatment is appropriate, an in-person appointment is scheduled at the most suitable location — coordinated entirely by your Patient Liaison, with detailed instructions before your visit.

06

Treatment Planning & Care

During your visit, Dr. Peaches reviews your history and imaging, performs any necessary examination, answers remaining questions, and finalizes recommendations. When appropriate, some patients undergo treatment during the same visit.

07

Ongoing Support

Healthcare shouldn't end after a consultation. Your Patient Liaison remains available for scheduling, follow-ups, records, recovery guidance, and treatment coordination — ensuring you understand your plan and feel supported throughout.

What To Expect

A personalized approach to your health

Dr. Peaches believes exceptional care starts with listening. The goal of your consultation is not simply to discuss symptoms — it is to understand the bigger picture and develop a strategy that aligns with your health, lifestyle, and long-term goals.

Orthopedic Care

Joint injuries, arthritis, tendon disorders, and sports-related conditions.

Spine & Pelvic Conditions

Neck pain, back pain, sciatica, degenerative conditions, and minimally invasive options.

Regenerative Medicine

Orthobiologic and regenerative therapies when appropriate.

Sports Medicine

Return-to-sport planning, performance optimization, and injury recovery.

Wellness & Longevity

Hair restoration, hormone optimization, sexual wellness, weight management, and healthy aging.

Honest Guidance

Every recommendation is individualized based on your symptoms, imaging, goals, and overall health.
