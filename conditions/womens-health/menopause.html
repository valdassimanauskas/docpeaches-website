Menopause

Understanding Menopause

Menopause is a natural stage of life that every woman experiences.

While menopause is a normal biological transition, the physical, hormonal, and emotional changes that accompany it can affect many aspects of health and well-being.

Many women seek answers because they notice changes such as:

  • Weight gain
  • Fatigue
  • Sleep disruption
  • Hot flashes
  • Reduced libido
  • Mood changes
  • Brain fog
  • Difficulty maintaining muscle mass
  • Reduced recovery

For some women, symptoms are mild. For others, menopause can significantly impact quality of life, relationships, work performance, exercise, and overall wellness.

At Dr. Peaches, menopause is approached through the lens of healthy aging, hormonal health, metabolic health, sexual wellness, performance, and longevity.

What Is Menopause?

Menopause is defined as the point when a woman has gone twelve consecutive months without a menstrual period.

Menopause occurs because the ovaries gradually reduce production of reproductive hormones, including estrogen and progesterone.

This transition is a normal part of aging.

The average age of menopause in the United States is approximately 51 years old, although every woman experiences menopause differently.

What Happens During Menopause?

Hormonal changes during menopause affect multiple systems throughout the body.

These changes may influence:

  • Energy levels
  • Sleep quality
  • Body composition
  • Sexual wellness
  • Mood
  • Recovery
  • Bone health
  • Metabolic health

Because hormones affect so many aspects of health, menopause symptoms often extend far beyond reproductive changes alone.

Common Symptoms Of Menopause

Women experience menopause differently, but common symptoms may include:

Hot Flashes

Sudden feelings of warmth that may affect the face, neck, chest, and upper body.

Night Sweats

Episodes of sweating during sleep that may disrupt rest and recovery.

Sleep Disturbances

Difficulty falling asleep, staying asleep, or feeling rested.

Fatigue

Persistent low energy and reduced stamina.

Weight Gain

Many women notice increased abdominal fat and changes in body composition.

Brain Fog

Difficulty concentrating, remembering information, or maintaining focus.

Mood Changes

Changes in emotional well-being may occur during hormonal transitions.

Reduced Libido

Changes in sexual desire are common during menopause.

Vaginal Dryness

Hormonal changes may affect vaginal health and comfort.

Menopause And Weight Gain

One of the most common concerns during menopause is weight gain.

Many women report:

  • Increased belly fat
  • Difficulty losing weight
  • Reduced muscle mass
  • Slower metabolism

These changes often reflect a combination of:

  • Hormonal shifts
  • Aging
  • Changes in body composition
  • Reduced muscle mass
  • Metabolic changes

Weight management during menopause often requires a different approach than it did earlier in life.

Menopause And Fatigue

Many women describe menopause as a time when their energy levels suddenly change.

Common concerns include:

  • Feeling exhausted despite adequate sleep
  • Reduced motivation
  • Poor recovery
  • Increased fatigue during daily activities

Because fatigue can have multiple contributing factors, a comprehensive evaluation is often important.

Menopause And Sexual Wellness

Sexual health remains an important component of overall wellness.

Many women report changes involving:

Libido

Reduced sexual desire.

Comfort

Changes in vaginal health and comfort during intimacy.

Confidence

Concerns that affect relationships and quality of life.

Intimacy

Physical and emotional changes that influence sexual wellness.

These concerns are common and deserve thoughtful discussion.

Menopause And Hormonal Health

Hormonal changes are at the center of menopause.

Changes in:

  • Estrogen
  • Progesterone
  • Testosterone
  • Other hormone systems

may influence how women feel physically and emotionally during this transition.

Understanding hormonal health is often an important component of menopause care.

Menopause And Muscle Loss

Many women notice changes in body composition during menopause.

Common concerns include:

  • Loss of muscle tone
  • Reduced strength
  • Increased body fat
  • Reduced physical performance

Preserving muscle mass becomes increasingly important for:

  • Metabolic health
  • Mobility
  • Longevity
  • Healthy aging

Menopause And Healthy Aging

Menopause often marks the beginning of a new phase of health and wellness.

Many women want to:

  • Stay active
  • Maintain energy
  • Preserve strength
  • Support longevity
  • Optimize sexual wellness
  • Continue exercising
  • Enjoy a high quality of life

Healthy aging and menopause are closely connected.

Treatment Considerations During Menopause

Treatment recommendations vary depending on symptoms, health history, and personal goals.

Discussions may include:

Lifestyle Optimization

Supporting sleep, recovery, exercise, and nutrition.

Weight Management

Addressing body composition and metabolic health.

Hormonal Evaluation

Assessing hormone-related symptoms when appropriate.

Sexual Wellness

Supporting intimacy, confidence, and overall well-being.

Performance And Longevity Strategies

Helping women remain active, healthy, and engaged throughout life.

Treatment recommendations should always be individualized.

Why Is Evaluation Important?

No two women experience menopause the same way.

Before discussing treatment options, it is important to understand:

  • Symptoms
  • Health history
  • Lifestyle factors
  • Hormonal health
  • Metabolic health
  • Sexual wellness concerns
  • Personal goals

A comprehensive evaluation helps guide personalized recommendations.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is menopause?

Menopause is the point when a woman has gone twelve consecutive months without a menstrual period.

What age does menopause usually occur?

The average age is approximately 51 years old, although timing varies between individuals.

Can menopause cause weight gain?

Many women experience changes in body composition and weight during menopause.

Why am I so tired during menopause?

Hormonal changes, sleep disruption, recovery challenges, and other factors may contribute to fatigue.

Can menopause affect libido?

Changes in hormones may influence sexual desire and overall sexual wellness.

Is menopause a disease?

No.

Menopause is a normal biological transition that occurs as part of aging.
