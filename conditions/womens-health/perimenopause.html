Women's Health

Perimenopause

Understanding Perimenopause

Many women begin experiencing symptoms years before menopause officially occurs.

They may notice:

  • Weight gain
  • Fatigue
  • Brain fog
  • Mood changes
  • Sleep disturbances
  • Reduced libido
  • Changes in menstrual cycles
  • Increased anxiety
  • Difficulty recovering from exercise

Often, women are told that their symptoms are simply a normal part of getting older.

In reality, many of these changes may be associated with perimenopause, the transitional period leading up to menopause.

At Dr. Peaches, perimenopause is approached through the lens of hormonal health, metabolic health, sexual wellness, healthy aging, and longevity.

The goal is to help women understand what is happening in their bodies and develop individualized strategies to support health and quality of life during this transition.

What Is Perimenopause?

Perimenopause refers to the years leading up to menopause.

During this transition, hormone levels begin to fluctuate as the ovaries gradually produce less estrogen and progesterone.

Perimenopause can begin years before menopause occurs.

For many women, symptoms start during their:

  • Late 30s
  • 40s
  • Early 50s

The duration varies significantly from person to person.

Perimenopause vs. Menopause

Many women use these terms interchangeably, but they are different.

Perimenopause

The transitional phase leading up to menopause when hormone levels fluctuate and symptoms may develop.

Menopause

Officially occurs after twelve consecutive months without a menstrual period.

Perimenopause often lasts several years before menopause is reached.

Common Symptoms Of Perimenopause

Perimenopause affects every woman differently.

Common symptoms include:

Irregular Periods

Changes in cycle length, flow, and frequency.

Fatigue

Reduced energy and increased exhaustion.

Sleep Disturbances

Difficulty falling asleep or staying asleep.

Brain Fog

Difficulty concentrating, focusing, or remembering information.

Mood Changes

Many women report increased irritability, anxiety, or emotional fluctuations.

Weight Gain

Particularly around the abdomen and midsection.

Reduced Libido

Changes in sexual desire and intimacy.

Hot Flashes

Episodes of warmth that may begin before menopause itself.

Night Sweats

Symptoms that interfere with sleep and recovery.

Why Does Perimenopause Happen?

Perimenopause occurs because of natural changes in reproductive hormone production.

Hormones that may fluctuate include:

  • Estrogen
  • Progesterone
  • Testosterone
  • Other endocrine hormones

These fluctuations can affect multiple systems throughout the body.

Perimenopause And Weight Gain

One of the most common concerns during perimenopause is unexpected weight gain.

Many women report:

  • Increased belly fat
  • Difficulty losing weight
  • Reduced muscle tone
  • Changes in body composition

These changes may be influenced by:

  • Hormonal fluctuations
  • Aging
  • Reduced muscle mass
  • Metabolic changes
  • Lifestyle factors

Weight management often requires a different strategy during perimenopause than it did earlier in life.

Perimenopause And Fatigue

Many women describe feeling:

  • Constantly tired
  • Drained
  • Less motivated
  • Unable to recover like they once did

Fatigue during perimenopause may be influenced by:

  • Sleep disruption
  • Hormonal fluctuations
  • Stress
  • Recovery challenges
  • Metabolic health

Because fatigue has many potential causes, comprehensive evaluation is important.

Perimenopause And Brain Fog

Brain fog is one of the most frustrating symptoms reported during perimenopause.

Women often describe:

  • Difficulty concentrating
  • Forgetfulness
  • Mental fatigue
  • Reduced focus

These symptoms can affect:

  • Work performance
  • Daily productivity
  • Confidence
  • Quality of life

Perimenopause And Sexual Wellness

Hormonal fluctuations may affect:

Libido

Changes in sexual desire.

Intimacy

Changes in comfort, confidence, and satisfaction.

Vaginal Health

Changes that may affect sexual wellness.

Relationships

Symptoms may influence emotional and physical intimacy.

Sexual wellness remains an important component of overall health.

Perimenopause And Exercise

Many women notice that exercise feels different during perimenopause.

Common concerns include:

  • Slower recovery
  • Difficulty building muscle
  • Increased soreness
  • Reduced performance

Maintaining strength and muscle mass becomes increasingly important during this phase of life.

Perimenopause And Healthy Aging

Perimenopause is not simply a reproductive transition.

It is also a major health transition.

Many women want to:

  • Maintain energy
  • Preserve muscle
  • Support longevity
  • Stay active
  • Maintain sexual wellness
  • Improve body composition

Healthy aging strategies often begin during perimenopause.

Treatment Considerations During Perimenopause

Treatment recommendations depend on symptoms, health history, and individual goals.

Discussions may include:

Lifestyle Optimization

Supporting sleep, nutrition, exercise, and recovery.

Weight Management

Addressing body composition and metabolic health.

Hormonal Evaluation

Assessing hormone-related symptoms when appropriate.

Sexual Wellness

Supporting intimacy and quality of life.

Performance And Longevity Strategies

Helping women remain active, strong, and healthy.

Treatment recommendations should always be individualized.

Why Is Evaluation Important?

Perimenopause symptoms often overlap with:

  • Thyroid concerns
  • Stress
  • Sleep disorders
  • Metabolic dysfunction
  • Hormonal imbalances

A comprehensive evaluation helps identify contributing factors and guide personalized recommendations.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is perimenopause?

Perimenopause is the transitional period leading up to menopause during which hormone levels fluctuate and symptoms may develop.

What age does perimenopause start?

Many women begin experiencing symptoms during their late 30s or 40s, although timing varies.

How long does perimenopause last?

The duration varies significantly between individuals and may last several years.

Can perimenopause cause weight gain?

Many women experience changes in body composition and weight during perimenopause.

Why am I so tired during perimenopause?

Hormonal fluctuations, sleep disruption, stress, and recovery challenges may all contribute.

Can perimenopause affect libido?

Yes.

Hormonal changes may influence sexual desire, intimacy, and overall sexual wellness.

The Dr. Peaches Perspective

Many women spend years struggling with symptoms before realizing they may be experiencing perimenopause.

The good news is that understanding the transition often helps women take proactive steps to support their health.

The goal is not simply managing symptoms.

The goal is helping women maintain energy, strength, confidence, sexual wellness, body composition, and quality of life during this important stage of life.

Perimenopause is a transition—not a decline.

With the right strategy, women can continue thriving physically, mentally, and emotionally for decades to come.
