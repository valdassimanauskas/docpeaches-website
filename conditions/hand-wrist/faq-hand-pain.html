FAQ Hand Pain

Frequently Asked Questions About Hand Pain

Hand pain is one of the most common musculoskeletal complaints affecting adults of all ages.

Because we use our hands throughout the day for work, exercise, sports, hobbies, and everyday activities, even minor symptoms can become frustrating.

Many different conditions can cause hand pain, stiffness, weakness, numbness, or loss of function.

Below are some of the most common questions patients ask about hand pain.

Why Does My Hand Hurt?

Hand pain may originate from several different structures, including:

  • Joints
  • Tendons
  • Ligaments
  • Muscles
  • Nerves
  • Bones

Common causes include:

  • Thumb arthritis
  • Trigger Finger
  • Carpal Tunnel Syndrome
  • Tendon disorders
  • Arthritis
  • Sports injuries

An accurate diagnosis is often the first step toward identifying appropriate treatment options.

Why Does My Thumb Hurt?

Thumb pain is one of the most common reasons patients seek evaluation.

Several conditions may contribute, including:

  • Thumb arthritis
  • Tendon disorders
  • Overuse injuries
  • Sports-related conditions

The location and pattern of symptoms often help guide diagnosis.

Why Does My Thumb Hurt When I Grip Things?

Gripping places substantial force through the thumb and hand.

Pain during gripping may be associated with:

  • Thumb arthritis
  • Tendon disorders
  • Overuse conditions
  • Other hand conditions

A comprehensive evaluation can help identify the source of symptoms.

Why Does My Finger Click?

Patients often describe:

"My finger clicks every time I bend it."

Clicking is commonly associated with Trigger Finger, although other conditions may contribute.

Why Does My Finger Get Stuck?

Locking or catching of a finger is one of the hallmark symptoms of Trigger Finger.

Patients may notice:

  • Clicking
  • Catching
  • Locking
  • Difficulty straightening the finger

Persistent symptoms should be evaluated.

What Is Trigger Finger?

Trigger Finger is a condition involving irritation of a tendon that helps move the finger.

Common symptoms include:

  • Clicking
  • Locking
  • Pain
  • Stiffness
  • Reduced hand function

Why Are My Hands Weak?

Weakness may occur because of:

  • Arthritis
  • Tendon disorders
  • Nerve conditions
  • Pain-related compensation
  • Previous injuries

The underlying cause varies from patient to patient.

Why Do My Hands Hurt In The Morning?

Morning stiffness may occur with:

  • Arthritis
  • Tendon disorders
  • Joint-related conditions
  • Other inflammatory conditions

The timing and pattern of symptoms often provide important diagnostic clues.

Why Do My Hands Fall Asleep At Night?

Nighttime numbness is commonly associated with Carpal Tunnel Syndrome.

Patients frequently report:

"I wake up and have to shake my hand out."

Persistent numbness should be evaluated.

What Is Carpal Tunnel Syndrome?

Carpal Tunnel Syndrome involves irritation of the median nerve as it travels through the wrist.

Common symptoms include:

  • Numbness
  • Tingling
  • Hand weakness
  • Nighttime symptoms

Why Am I Dropping Things?

Some patients notice:

  • Reduced grip strength
  • Difficulty holding objects
  • Increased clumsiness

These symptoms may occur when hand function becomes affected by pain, weakness, or nerve-related conditions.

Why Does My Hand Cramp?

Hand cramping may occur for many reasons, including:

  • Muscle fatigue
  • Overuse
  • Repetitive activities
  • Neuromuscular conditions

Persistent or worsening symptoms should be evaluated.

Can Golf Cause Hand Pain?

Yes.

Golf places substantial demands on the hands and wrists.

Symptoms may be associated with:

  • Arthritis
  • Tendon disorders
  • Overuse conditions
  • Nerve-related conditions

Can Pickleball Cause Hand Pain?

Yes.

Pickleball involves repetitive gripping and impact forces that may contribute to symptoms in some individuals.

What Is Thumb Arthritis?

Thumb arthritis refers to changes involving the joint located at the base of the thumb.

Common symptoms include:

  • Pain at the base of the thumb
  • Weakness
  • Difficulty gripping
  • Difficulty opening jars

What Is De Quervain's Tenosynovitis?

De Quervain's Tenosynovitis is a tendon-related condition affecting the thumb side of the wrist.

Common symptoms include:

  • Thumb-side wrist pain
  • Pain with lifting
  • Pain with gripping
  • Pain with twisting motions

Do I Need An MRI?

Not always.

Many hand conditions can be evaluated effectively through:

  • Medical history
  • Physical examination
  • X-rays

MRI studies may be appropriate in selected situations.

What Is The Difference Between An MRI And X-Ray?

X-Ray

Primarily evaluates:

  • Bone
  • Joint alignment
  • Arthritis

MRI

Provides additional information regarding:

  • Tendons
  • Ligaments
  • Nerves
  • Soft tissues

Both studies may play important roles in the diagnostic process.

Does Hand Pain Always Require Surgery?

No.

Many patients explore non-surgical treatment options depending on:

  • Diagnosis
  • Symptoms
  • Functional limitations
  • Activity goals

Treatment recommendations should always be individualized.

When Should I See A Specialist For Hand Pain?

You should consider evaluation if:

  • Pain persists.
  • Gripping becomes difficult.
  • Fingers lock or click.
  • Weakness develops.
  • Numbness develops.
  • Previous treatment has not provided sufficient improvement.

Understanding the diagnosis is often the first step toward understanding your options.

The Dr. Peaches Perspective

Hand pain is common.

The challenge is determining why the hand hurts.

Because multiple structures can create similar symptoms, obtaining an accurate diagnosis is often the most important step in the treatment process.

Whether the goal is golfing, pickleball, fitness, work, travel, hobbies, or simply performing daily activities comfortably, treatment should be individualized and centered around helping patients maintain the activities that matter most.
